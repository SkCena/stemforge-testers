# Brief: StemForge (offline audio stem splitter, no account)

Read AGENT_BRIEF.md first: its tools, rules and final report all apply.
Only the goal changes, because StemForge needs no account and works offline.

- Out of scope: AnkiWeb-style accounts — there are none. Never sign up, log in,
  or send data anywhere. Decline any permission prompt you do not need.
- Out of scope: actually splitting or cleaning audio. This phone has no music
  files on it, and the AI models are large downloads (30-165 MB) — do NOT start
  any model download. The split itself is tested on a real phone separately.
- Your goal instead, as your persona:
  1. Go through the first-run tutorial to the very end. Note whether Skip works
     and whether every page is readable at your text size.
  2. On the Projects screen, read everything, then tap the Import button.
     The phone has no music files: note exactly what the file picker shows and
     whether you can get back without getting stuck.
  3. Open the Models tab (bottom bar). Read every model row: name, size and
     status must all be visible and readable. Note what the app says about the
     4-stem model and this phone's memory. Do not start a download.
  4. Open the Settings tab. Open "How StemForge works" (the tutorial again),
     go back, and read the About card — check the version line fits.
  5. Go back to Projects and leave the app, then reopen it: the tutorial must
     NOT appear again, and the app must reopen on Projects.
- Everywhere: report any text cut off, overlapping, or running off the screen,
  any button you cannot reach or read, and anything that looks broken — always
  with the name of the screen where you saw it.
